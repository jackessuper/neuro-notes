# Welcome 👋

Neurology has a reputation for being hard. Most of that reputation comes from the first few weeks, when every referral sounds like a localisation exam and every stroke call feels like it needs someone more senior than you.

This site is the stuff you actually need on the ward and on call, written down in one place: how to build a differential, how to localise, what to do on a stroke call, how to read the scans, how to do and interpret an LP, and how to handle the headaches that fill the clinic.
